# Overseas study model

Static, free game logic across18countries. Not immigration advice, actual offers, or live fee quotes.

## Choices and transactions
Country and named university, one of eight non-clinical modelled subjects, bachelor/master/PhD, cash plus savings/parents/awarded tuition scholarship. Bachelor duration follows the country programme model. Master takes24months and PhD36months. Master requires a first degree and PhD requires master. Concurrent study and duplicate credentials are blocked. English certification or fluency is required, with an English-home-country simplification. Not all real institutions offer these English-taught courses; these are explicitly game pathways.

International tuition is an illustrative country budget, floored by the named institution's existing game tuition and weighted by subject. It is never substituted with a domestic public subsidy. Undergraduate first year is paid upfront and credited for12months. Later years are self-billed monthly or renewed from actual living parents' savings; failed parent renewal falls back to self-pay with journal warning. Postgraduate full tuition is prepaid. The first-year living budget is a proof-of-funds buffer, not an extra charge. Modelled maintenance escrow, where used, debits once and releases monthly. Travel/permit cost is a labelled game budget, not a claim about current government fees.

A separate fictional tuition award has one35%review per destination/level/year,85secondary-score and75smarts gates, language and degree prerequisites. A win covers tuition only, no stipend. Never assume a high score is already an award. Cash, savings and parent debits precede relocation. Business-deferred moves persist a plain-data course descriptor and cannot be charged twice. Student permit lasts course duration plus3months. Post-study route is a simplified game transition, not a legal entitlement or permanent residency.
