# Education model, November 2026

Static, free game data. No network requests or runtime AI.

Country ordinary bachelor defaults replace a single worldwide duration. Specialist years are game study years, not a licence to practise. Existing enrolled saves retain their contracted length and annual charge. Country profiles do not certify each named university course. Ordinary subjects remain deliberately modelled pathways.

Clinical coverage is narrower than the real world: only checked Cairo/UCL/NUS/Harvard medicine and NUS dentistry examples are currently enabled. Other clinical programmes are not modelled yet, rather than inventing offerings. UCL medicine is six years; Cairo medicine uses six years before the 2018 entry change and five thereafter, excluding the separate internship. Harvard medicine requires an earlier game degree. Other graduate-entry US/Canadian professional pathways also require an earlier degree in this simplified system.

All amounts are explicitly game budgets. Public statutory references use a local eligible-student assumption. Course factors elsewhere are simulation estimates, not official tuition. Living costs, residency-specific fees, grants with service obligations, licensing and specialist-school exceptions are not fully simulated. Entry scores outside checked Egyptian tables are game selectivity scores. Ownership does not automatically reduce admission difficulty.

Duplicate institution aliases preserve old admission IDs. KAUST is excluded from the school-leaver list because its offering is graduate study. Sciences Po is labelled public institute/private foundation, Chalmers foundation university, Singapore autonomous universities, and UK publicly funded independent institutions.

## State and funding audit
School-age controls no longer appear after school. Enrolled students see the current degree; graduates see compact history and extra study. Withdrawal has an explicit non-refund warning and confirmation. Parent funding debits living parents' actual savings for one year and renews annually if enough remains. If renewal fails, billing changes to self-pay and a journal warning is recorded. Self-pay bills monthly, with a first-year cash buffer required. Country-matched undergraduate scholarship awards change tuition coverage, including while enrolled. Postgraduate scholarship buttons are hidden because no funding settlement was wired. Finance offers ordinary personal loans, not a student-loan product. Master and doctorate study charge the displayed one-off game fee, progress monthly and require prior study. Association membership is a game benefit, not a real licence.

Remaining limits: school tuition and tutoring still use older simplified family coverage, not the new university parent ledger. Generic ordinary-subject catalogues and many professional credential rules remain simplified. The broad720screen render audit is not proof that every button or life outcome is correct. Floor-plan circulation repair is still pending.
